About My Fitness Lifestyle

Fitness has always been a huge part of my life. Whether it's hitting the gym, playing basketball, or just getting outside, staying active keeps me energized and focused. This page is dedicated to the different ways I've been able to stay in shape and the routines that have worked for me.

My Weekly Routine

Here's how I typically structure my week to stay on top of fitness:

  1. Strength Training (3x/week)
    • Bench press & chest flies
    • Squats and deadlifts
    • Pull-ups and rows
    • Core work — planks, cable crunches
  2. Cardio & Sports (2x/week)
    • Basketball pickup games
    • Tennis with friends
    • 30-minute runs
  3. Recovery Days (2x/week)
    • Stretching and mobility work
    • Light walks outside
    • Good nutrition and rest
